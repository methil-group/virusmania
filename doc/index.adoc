== Gameplay

For this game jam, our goal is to work primarly on the gameplay, developping it and making it fun
and enjoyable. To do that, we decide to sacrifice the game naration and history to have more time
to spend on the gameplay loop, actions and diversity.

You can find all the details about the gameplay in on the decated xref:./gameplay/index.adoc[Gameplay section]
of this documentation.

== Validation

La xref:./gameplay/validation.adoc[procédure de validation] décrit les tests PlayMode,
la construction de la version Windows de développement et le parcours de vérification manuelle.

La xref:./gameplay/visual-review/rapport.adoc[revue visuelle] rassemble les captures d'écran,
le schéma de la boucle de gameplay et les améliorations de lisibilité à poursuivre.
Ouvrir link:./gameplay/visual-review/index.html[la galerie interactive hors ligne] pour les parcourir.
